Notes

- Fonts are bundled unmodified for non-commercial, sadaqah-purpose Quran
  reading inside this PWA. They are NOT sold and are NOT stripped of credits.

  re-mapping of any font).
- If you are a rights holder of any font and object to this non-commercial
  bundling, open an issue and the font will be removed immediately.
