# Craft rules

What separates a screen that looks finished from one that looks rough. The mockup builders follow these, and the reviewer checks them. Each one can be checked by looking.

## Order and space

1. **One thing leads.** On every screen, one element is clearly the most important. Show importance with weight, colour and space, not only with size. Make secondary things quieter.
2. **One spacing scale.** Every gap comes from the same short list of sizes. Things that belong together sit closer than things that do not.
3. **Everything lines up.** Edges and text baselines line up with something else on the screen.
4. **One main action per screen.** One button looks like the main one. The others are plainer.
5. **Not everything needs a box.** Group with space and headings first. Use a border or a card only when it adds something.

## Colour and type

6. **Colour means something.** A colour that marks a status is used for that status only. Status is never shown by colour alone: it also has a word or an icon.
7. **Text is readable.** Body text has strong contrast with its background. No grey text on a coloured background.
8. **Few sizes, clear jumps.** A short list of text sizes, each used for one job. Numbers in tables and tiles use figures of equal width, so the digits line up.

## Data

9. **A number is never alone.** It has a unit, a time, and where it helps, a comparison: against yesterday, against a limit.
10. **Tables read easily.** Numbers are right-aligned, text is left-aligned, a heading lines up with its column, decimals are consistent, and the lines between rows are light.
11. **The freshness of data is visible.** A screen that shows live values says when they were last updated.

## States and behaviour

12. **Every state is designed.** Empty, loading, error and a very long value each have a designed look. An empty screen says what goes here and how to add it. An error says what went wrong and what to do.
13. **Things you can press look pressable,** and react when hovered, focused and pressed. Keyboard focus is always visible.
14. **Touch targets are big enough:** at least 44 pixels high on a phone. Text in form fields is at least 16 pixels on a phone.
15. **It works at every width it needs.** Nothing overflows, overlaps or is cut off, and there is no sideways scrolling.
16. **Motion is rare and quick.** It shows that something changed. It never decorates, and it is switched off for people who ask their device for less motion.

## Words

17. **Buttons say what they do:** "Save alert rule", not "Submit". The same action has the same name everywhere.
18. **Real content.** The app's real names and realistic values, including a long one and an empty one. Never placeholder text.
